# Third-Party Notices

## interviewstreet/hiring-agent

Carivyo's local hiring-manager evaluation adapts the structured extraction,
provider-agnostic local inference, evidence-backed scoring, and fairness ideas
from [`interviewstreet/hiring-agent`](https://github.com/interviewstreet/hiring-agent),
audited at commit `1c6cae5dded78a3cc08b124b9796e014a309531d` (2026-07-22).

Carivyo does not vendor the upstream application or its dependencies. In
particular, its internship-specific and GitHub-heavy scoring rubric is not used
as a hiring decision, and its PyMuPDF extraction dependency is not incorporated.
